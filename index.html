BUILT TO GROW

FUTURE ROADMAP

Designed so CricketVerse can grow after publishing, taking feedback, and adding real online services.

01 · LIVE ONLINE MATCHES

Friend invites, room codes, matchmaking, reconnect support and anti-cheat — requires a real-time game server.

02 · PLAYER ACCOUNTS & CLOUD SAVES

Secure sign-in, cross-device progress, player profiles and recovery — requires backend and database.

03 · PUBLIC REVIEWS & COMMUNITY

Published reviews, ratings, reports, moderation and developer replies — requires server-side storage.

04 · SHAREABLE GAME LINK

Deploy to hosting and share a public HTTPS link; players can open it in a browser. Multiplayer still needs servers.

05 · CAREER & LEAGUE SIMULATION

Seasons, transfers, auctions, schedules, points tables, playoffs, player progression and save slots.

06 · PERFORMANCE & ACCESSIBILITY

Low/medium/high graphics, mobile controls, controller support, language options and device testing.

Roadmap items are plans, not currently active services. A static ZIP or GitHub Pages site alone cannot provide real-time multiplayer, public review storage, accounts, or cloud saves.
